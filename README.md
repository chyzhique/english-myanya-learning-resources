# English Learning Resources (A0–C1)

Free English learning resources for self-taught adults. Not another chatbot dump. Not a streak counter. A clear map you can follow when you want to **learn English** at your own pace — from first words to confident conversation.

If you search for how to learn English, English practice online, or an English study plan you can keep after work, start here. Read one guide. Speak out loud. Come back for the next piece of the path.

**A course. Not a chat.** That line is the filter for everything in this repo.

---

## Who this is for

- Adults learning English alone (kitchen table, not classroom)
- People who want **English for beginners** without babyish apps
- Learners aiming at **British English** for Europe, travel, or work
- Anyone stuck between random YouTube videos and a boring textbook

You will find English grammar, English vocabulary, English conversation patterns, and English speaking practice you can do without a partner.

## How to use this hub

1. **Pick your level.** Start in [beginner/](beginner/) if you need English lessons for beginners (A0–A2).
2. **Fix one grammar hole.** Open [grammar/](grammar/) when a form keeps breaking your sentences.
3. **Steal phrases you will actually say.** Use [vocabulary/](vocabulary/) for everyday English phrases and English phrases for real life.
4. **Open your mouth.** Use [speaking/](speaking/) for how to practice English speaking alone, including English speaking practice for beginners.
5. **Tune the sound.** Use [pronunciation/](pronunciation/) for British English pronunciation practice.
6. **Keep a plan.** Use [resources/](resources/) for English learning websites, apps worth testing, and a simple English study plan.

Fifteen honest minutes beat a heroic weekend. That is English self study that sticks.

## Suggested English learning roadmap

A practical English learning path if you are building the plan yourself:

| Stage | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **A0** | Sounds + 20 survival phrases + 15 minutes a day |
| **A1** | Question frames + 60-second recordings |
| **A2** | Past forms + shop/travel dialogues |
| **B1** | Opinions, stories about your week, longer listening |
| **B2–C1** | Precision, nuance, work and debate language |

That is an **A0 to C1 English** sketch, not a promise of speed. Consistency beats intensity. If choosing the next lesson is what breaks you, a **structured English course** / **self paced English course** on [MyAnya](https://myanya.app) keeps the order fixed while you still do the work.

## British English note

Examples lean **British English** where it helps real life in Europe: spelling, rhythm, and street forms like *You alright?* or *Fancy a coffee?*. You will still understand American media. Pick one spelling system for writing and stay steady.

## English practice that actually counts

Good English language practice looks like this:

- You speak or write something specific
- You check one or two mistakes
- You repeat tomorrow

Bad practice looks like endless chat with no map, or tapping an app for a streak. This hub is built for the first kind — English lessons and English exercises you can finish, then reuse.

For feedback without booking a human tutor, Anya on [myanya.app](https://myanya.app) checks speaking and writing inside a guided path. Still a course. Still not a chatbot.
