# The Ultimate English Learning Resources (A0-C1)

A massive index of guides, grammar notes, and street English for people who learn English alone. No textbook theatre. No streak counters. Just English learning resources you can use this week.

This repo is a map. Pick a folder. Read one guide. Practice out loud. Come back when you need the next piece of the English learning path from A0 to C1 English.

> **Looking for a structured way to learn?**

> If you don't want to build your own study plan and piece together free resources, **[MyAnya](https://myanya.app)** turns English practice into a guided path from A0 onward. It’s a course, not a chat. You try, she marks your speaking and writing.

## How to use this hub

1. Start in **beginner/** if you are new or restarting.
2. Use **grammar/** when a form keeps confusing you.
3. Steal phrases from **vocabulary/** and say them aloud.
4. Open **speaking/** when you need English speaking practice and English pronunciation practice alone.

British English shows up in examples where it helps you sound natural in everyday situations. The goal is a structured English course feel, even when you are piecing free material together yourself.
