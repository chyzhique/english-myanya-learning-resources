# A1 English: From Survival Phrases to Simple Conversations

You know a few words. You can say hello. Now you need short exchanges that feel real. These English lessons for beginners at A1 focus on questions, answers, and everyday English phrases for work, shops, and neighbours.

A1 is control, not advanced grammar. Ask for things. Talk about your day. Understand slow, clear speech.

## Five actionable steps

1. **Master question frames.** Drill: Where is…? How much…? What time…? Can I…? Do you have…? Write five real questions for this week. Say them aloud. English practice with a purpose.
2. **Build mini answers.** "Where is the station?" → "It's on the left. About five minutes." Short answers beat long, broken sentences.
3. **Expand kitchen-table vocabulary.** Food, transport, work, weather — grouped by your life. English phrases for real life stick; random lists do not.
4. **Do five-minute speaking drills.** One topic a day: my morning, my job, my weekend. Speak sixty seconds. Record. Fix two mistakes. Repeat. That is how to practice English speaking alone at A1.
5. **Rewrite one short dialogue.** Change names, places, times. Keep British English spelling if that is your Europe target. Read it out loud for English pronunciation practice.

## Weekly shape

Monday–Friday: 20 minutes (questions + speaking). Saturday: review phrases. Sunday: one longer beginner listen. One notebook and one audio source beat ten apps. Clear English learning path over scattered taps.
