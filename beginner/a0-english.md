# A0 English: First Steps for Absolute Beginners

You are starting from zero. That is fine. These English lessons for beginners assume you know almost no English yet. Keep sessions short. Aim for clarity, not speed.

A0 is about sound, survival phrases, and a tiny daily habit. Skip long grammar books. You need words for a shop, a bus, or a simple greeting.

## Five actionable steps

1. **Learn the alphabet and core sounds.** Ten minutes a day on letter names and basic vowels. Watch a short British English clip and repeat out loud. English pronunciation practice here is mouth muscle work.
2. **Memorise twenty survival phrases.** Hello, please, thank you, sorry, I don't understand, how much is this, where is the toilet, I need help. Write them down. Say each three times. English phrases for real life, not exam lines.
3. **Name ten things in your kitchen.** Cup. Plate. Water. Phone. Door. Point and say the word. Add one verb: I drink water. I open the door.
4. **Shadow one tiny dialogue.** Find a 30-second beginner clip. Play one line. Pause. Copy the rhythm. Once a day. Early English speaking practice without a partner.
5. **Write three sentences at night.** My name is… I live in… Today I… Simple English practice. Habit over perfect pages.

## Study rhythm

15 minutes sounds and phrases. 10 minutes shadowing. 5 minutes writing. Seven steady days beat one heroic weekend. That is self-paced English course logic even when you build the plan yourself.
