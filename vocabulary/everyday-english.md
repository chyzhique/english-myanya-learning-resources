# Everyday English Phrases for Real Life

Textbook English keeps you polite and distant. Everyday English phrases get you through a shop queue, a neighbour chat, and a work kitchen. Below are ten English phrases for real life, with a quick contrast so you hear the difference.

Focus here is British English where the street form is common in the UK and widely understood in Europe.

## 10 English phrases for real life

1. **You alright?**  
   Textbook: How are you?  
   Street: A casual hello. Often no long answer expected. "Yeah, you?" is enough.

2. **Cheers.**  
   Textbook: Thank you. / Goodbye.  
   Street: Thanks, or a soft goodbye after a small favour.

3. **Do you mind if I…?**  
   Textbook: May I…?  
   Street: Polite request that still sounds human. "Do you mind if I sit here?"

4. **I'm easy.**  
   Textbook: I don't mind. / Either is fine.  
   Street: When someone asks what you want to eat or watch.

5. **I'll get this.**  
   Textbook: I will pay.  
   Street: Paying for coffee or a round. Clear and natural.

6. **Sorry, have you got the time?**  
   Textbook: What time is it?  
   Street: Soft opener. Common British English rhythm.

7. **I'm knackered.**  
   Textbook: I am very tired.  
   Street: End-of-day honesty among friends or friendly colleagues.

8. **Fancy a coffee?**  
   Textbook: Would you like to have coffee?  
   Street: Short invite. Useful English practice for social plans.

9. **That rings a bell.**  
   Textbook: That sounds familiar.  
   Street: When a name or place is half-known.

10. **I'll leave you to it.**  
    Textbook: I will let you continue working.  
    Street: Polite exit from a conversation at work.

## How to train these

- Pick three phrases this week.
- Write one situation for each (shop, neighbour, office).
- Say the phrase aloud ten times.
- Record one short role-play for English pronunciation practice.
