# English Study Plan for Self-Taught Adults (A0–B1 Sketch)

You do not need a perfect system. You need a weekly English study plan you can keep after work. This is English self study for kitchen-table adults — clear blocks, no heroic weekends.

Use this as a sketch from A0 toward B1. Adjust times to your life. Fifteen honest minutes beat a cancelled two-hour block.

## Core weekly shape (any level)

| Block | Time | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Form | 15 min × 3 days | One English grammar focus |
| Phrases | 15 min × 2 days | Everyday English phrases / scene vocab |
| Speak | 10–15 min daily | English speaking practice (record or shadow) |
| Listen | 10 min × 3 days | Short British English clip |
| Review | 20 min Saturday | Same lines again — no new topics |

Sunday optional: light review or rest. Consistency is the product.

## A0 sketch (first weeks)

- Sounds + 20 survival phrases
- Kitchen nouns + three verbs a day
- Shadow one 30-second clip
- Write three sentences at night

See [A0 English](../beginner/a0-english.md).

## A1 sketch

- Question frames: Where / How much / Can I / Do you have
- Mini answers (two sentences)
- 60-second recordings: my morning, my job, my weekend
- One shop or neighbour dialogue

See [A1 English](../beginner/a1-english.md) and [everyday conversations](../speaking/everyday-conversations.md).

## A2 sketch

- Past simple for your real week
- Travel and restaurant phrases
- 90-second stories: what happened yesterday
- Link grammar pages (present simple, past simple, can/can't) to speech

See [A2 English](../beginner/a2-english.md), [travel](../vocabulary/travel-english.md), [restaurant](../vocabulary/restaurant-english.md).

## Early B1 sketch

- Opinions: I think / I'm not sure / It depends
- Longer listening (3–5 minutes) with notes
- Tell a story with past + reason + feeling
- Fix two recurring mistakes for a full week

Still keep British English spelling steady if that is your target.

## Sample week (A1–A2 hybrid)

- **Mon:** Present or past forms + 10 min speak  
- **Tue:** Travel or shop phrases + shadow  
- **Wed:** Record 60–90 seconds (weekend / yesterday)  
- **Thu:** Can/can't requests + role-play  
- **Fri:** Restaurant lines + pronunciation drill  
- **Sat:** Review notebook; rewrite one dialogue  
- **Sun:** Rest or light listen  

That is a practical English learning path without ten apps.

## When to switch to a structured English course

Stay on free guides while you can answer: *What am I studying this week?* Switch when:

- Choosing the next lesson eats the session
- You jump topics and never finish a form
- You want speaking and writing checked against a fixed path
- A0 to C1 English progress feels random, not staged

Then a self-paced English course helps. [MyAnya](https://myanya.app) is built for that job — a course, not a chat. You try. She marks speaking and writing. Keep this hub for reference. Use the guided path when the plan itself is the bottleneck.
